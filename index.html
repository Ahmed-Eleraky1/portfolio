Deal story

The car auction app nobody understood

14

days from first contact to signed deal

The problem

A clear idea, but nobody saw it

A client wanted to build a car auction app. The people he had spoken to couldn't picture his idea the way he did.

Step 1

Listened first

I let him walk me through how the auctions should work and asked questions until the bidding flow and his business goals were clear.

Step 2

Made it visible

I opened a similar auction platform with him and used it as a reference: what to keep, what to change, and what makes his app different.

Step 3

Scoped and closed

I turned the idea into a clear scope and a tailored technical and financial proposal, and we signed within 14 days.

Clients don't buy features. They buy being understood.
