A salesperson who speaks developer

I started as a front-end developer and moved into sales. Now I use both: I know how software gets built, and I know how to sell it.

I'm a Management Information Systems graduate from Mansoura, Egypt. I built E-Vital, a medical web platform, before moving into software sales in 2025. Since then I've sold mobile apps, websites, CRM and ERP systems and e-commerce platforms to clients in Saudi Arabia and Egypt.

Most clients come to me with an idea, not a spec. My job is to understand the business behind it, turn it into a scope the development team can build, price it fairly and close it, then stay with the client through handover so nothing gets lost between the sale and the delivery.
